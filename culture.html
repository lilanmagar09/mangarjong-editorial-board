Culture & Heritage

A digital space for documenting, preserving and sharing community culture and heritage.

Culture and heritage carry knowledge from one generation to another. They include practices, traditions, language, memories, knowledge and forms of community life.

This section of the Mangarjong Editorial Board website is intended to develop into a digital archive of verified cultural and heritage materials related to the Mangar community.

Areas of Documentation

Documentation Principle

Cultural information published on this website should be based on reliable community knowledge, research or documented sources. Different local practices and interpretations will be acknowledged where relevant.
