About Mangarjong Editorial Board

A publication platform dedicated to writing, research, culture, language and community knowledge.

About the Board

Mangarjong Editorial Board was established in 2018. Since its establishment, the Board has been working towards creating a platform for publication and the sharing of knowledge related to the Mangar community and its wider social and cultural context.

The Board provides a space for writers, researchers, contributors and community members to share their work, ideas and knowledge through publication.

Publication

Following its establishment, the Mangarjong Editorial Board began publishing a volume every year. These publications provide an important record of writings, research, community knowledge and matters connected with culture and identity.

Established: 2018
Publication: Annual volumes

Our Focus

The work of the Editorial Board focuses on publication, research, culture, language, heritage and community knowledge. The platform aims to provide space for documenting and sharing knowledge that is meaningful to the community.

The Board also seeks to encourage writing and research on subjects connected with the history, society, culture, language and contemporary concerns of the Mangar community.

Our Digital Archive

This website is being developed as a digital platform for the Mangarjong Editorial Board. It will provide access to published volumes, articles, contributors, cultural materials, language resources and future updates.

As the digital archive develops, published materials will be added to the website using verified information and original publication records.

Preserving Knowledge

Publication is not only a means of sharing information. It also helps preserve knowledge for future generations. Through its publications and digital archive, Mangarjong Editorial Board seeks to contribute to the documentation and preservation of community knowledge, cultural identity and intellectual work.
