Mangar Language

A developing digital space for documenting and preserving the Mangar language and related linguistic knowledge.

Language is an important part of cultural identity and community knowledge. This section is intended to develop into a digital resource for the documentation and preservation of the Mangar language.

The archive will include verified linguistic material, vocabulary, meanings, expressions and other language resources as they are documented and reviewed.
